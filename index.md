# ME 3330: Mechanical Vibrations — Teaching Sample

## Module: Single Degree of Freedom Vibrating Systems

This instructional module demonstrates how theory and scientific programming can be integrated within a single week of **ME 3330 (Mechanical Vibrations)**. 

Rather than treating traditional lectures and practical sessions as distinct silos, students derive the governing equations for a damped single-degree-of-freedom (SDOF) oscillator, explore parameter sensitivity computationally, and, where feasible, validate their model through physical experiments.

### Course Context
* **Target Course:** ME 3330 — Mechanical Vibrations (Senior/Graduate Elective)
* **Prerequisites:** ME 2120 (System Dynamics), ME 1120 (Dynamics)
* **Module Duration:** 2 Lecture Sessions (50 min each) + 1  Bench-Top Lab Activity

### Learning Objectives
By the end of this module, students will be able to:
1. **Formulate** the governing equation of motions for translational and rotational single degree of freedom (SDOF) systems using D'Alembert's Principle.
2. **Eliminate** reaction forces strategically by applying dynamic equilibrium about instantaneous centers.
3. **Reduce** multi-component mechanical assemblies into canonical single degree of freedom systems and apply linearization.
4. **Evaluate** the validity limits of linearized single degree of freedom models against exact nonlinear numerical simulations in Python
